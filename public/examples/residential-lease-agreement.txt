RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on May 1, 2024, between Metro Property Management LLC ("Landlord") and Alex Johnson ("Tenant").

1. PROPERTY DESCRIPTION

1.1 Premises: Landlord hereby leases to Tenant the residential property located at:
456 Oak Street, Apartment 3B
Seattle, Washington 98101

1.2 Included: Apartment includes kitchen appliances (refrigerator, stove, dishwasher), in-unit washer/dryer, one assigned parking space (#23), and access to building amenities (fitness center, rooftop deck, package room).

1.3 Excluded: Storage unit and additional parking spaces are not included but may be rented separately for $75/month and $150/month respectively.

2. LEASE TERM

2.1 Initial Term: This Lease begins on June 1, 2024, and ends on May 31, 2025 (12 months).

2.2 Move-In Date: Tenant may take possession on June 1, 2024, after 10:00 AM.

2.3 Move-Out Date: Tenant must vacate by May 31, 2025, by 12:00 PM (noon).

2.4 Renewal Option: Tenant has the option to renew for an additional 12 months by providing written notice at least 60 days before lease end (by April 1, 2025). Renewal rent may increase up to 7% or local rent control limits, whichever is lower.

2.5 Month-to-Month Conversion: If neither party terminates, this Lease automatically converts to month-to-month with 30 days notice required. Month-to-month rent will be 110% of the base rent ($2,750/month).

3. RENT AND FEES

3.1 Monthly Rent: $2,500 per month, due on the 1st of each month.

3.2 Payment Method: Rent must be paid through the tenant portal at www.metropropertymanagement.com/pay or by check delivered to office. Cash payments are not accepted.

3.3 Late Fees: Rent not received by the 5th of the month will incur a late fee of $75. An additional $10/day late fee applies starting on the 10th.

3.4 Returned Payment Fee: Returned checks or failed ACH payments incur a $50 fee.

3.5 Security Deposit: $2,500 (one month's rent) due before move-in. Deposit will be held in an interest-bearing account and returned within 21 days after move-out, less any deductions for damages beyond normal wear and tear.

3.6 Pet Deposit: $500 refundable pet deposit and $50/month pet rent per approved pet (maximum 2 pets).

3.7 Application Fee: $50 per adult (non-refundable), already paid.

3.8 Move-In Costs Summary:
- First Month's Rent: $2,500
- Security Deposit: $2,500
- Pet Deposit (if applicable): $500
- Total Move-In: $5,500 (or $5,000 without pet)

4. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

4.1 Tenant Pays: Electricity, gas, internet, cable TV, renter's insurance (required).

4.2 Landlord Pays: Water, sewer, trash collection, common area maintenance, property insurance.

4.3 Utility Transfer: Tenant must transfer utilities to their name within 5 days of move-in and maintain service throughout the Lease term.

4.4 Renter's Insurance: Tenant must maintain renter's insurance with minimum $100,000 liability coverage and provide proof within 10 days of move-in and annually thereafter.

5. USE AND OCCUPANCY

5.1 Occupants: Only the following persons may occupy the premises:
- Alex Johnson (Tenant)
- Maximum 2 additional occupants with Landlord approval

5.2 Guests: Guests may stay up to 10 consecutive days or 20 total days in any 60-day period. Extended guests require written approval and may trigger additional occupant fees.

5.3 Residential Use Only: Premises may only be used as a residence. No business activities allowed without prior written consent.

5.4 Subletting Prohibited: Tenant may not sublet, assign, or transfer the Lease without Landlord's written consent. Unauthorized subletting is grounds for immediate termination.

5.5 Short-Term Rentals: Listing the property on Airbnb, VRBO, or similar platforms is strictly prohibited and will result in $500/day fine and immediate lease termination.

6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

6.1 Landlord Responsibilities: Landlord will maintain structural elements, roof, HVAC system, plumbing, electrical systems, and appliances in working order.

6.2 Tenant Responsibilities: Tenant must:
- Keep premises clean and sanitary
- Replace light bulbs and batteries
- Pay for damage caused by negligence or misuse
- Report maintenance issues within 24 hours
- Replace HVAC filters quarterly (provided by Landlord)

6.3 Maintenance Requests: Submit requests through tenant portal or email maintenance@metropropertymanagement.com. Emergency maintenance (after hours): call (206) 555-0100.

6.4 Response Times:
- Emergency (no heat, major water leak): 4 hours
- Urgent (appliance failure, minor leak): 24 hours
- Routine (cosmetic issues): 5 business days

6.5 Entry for Repairs: Landlord may enter with 24 hours notice for routine repairs, or without notice for emergencies.

7. PROPERTY CONDITION AND INSPECTION

7.1 Move-In Inspection: Tenant must complete move-in inspection checklist within 5 days and submit to Landlord. Failure to report existing damage may result in charges at move-out.

7.2 Move-Out Inspection: Tenant may request a pre-move-out inspection 14 days before lease end to identify issues needing repair.

7.3 Final Inspection: Landlord will inspect within 3 days after move-out.

7.4 Cleaning Requirements: Premises must be professionally cleaned at move-out, including:
- Carpet steam cleaning (receipt required)
- All appliances cleaned
- Walls free of marks and holes
- Windows cleaned
Failure to clean results in deduction from security deposit ($250 minimum).

8. RULES AND REGULATIONS

8.1 Quiet Hours: 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM daily. Excessive noise may result in warnings and lease termination.

8.2 Smoking: Smoking (including vaping and cannabis) is prohibited inside the unit and within 25 feet of building entrances. Violation results in $250 fine per occurrence and full deposit forfeiture for smoke damage.

8.3 Pets: Maximum 2 pets allowed with approval. Prohibited breeds: Pit Bulls, Rottweilers, Dobermans, German Shepherds. Pets must be registered with building, vaccinated, and leashed in common areas. Unauthorized pets result in $500 fine and required removal within 7 days.

8.4 Parking: One assigned space (#23) included. Parking in others' spaces or unauthorized vehicles may be towed at owner's expense. Guest parking is first-come, first-served.

8.5 Alterations: No painting, wallpaper, or structural changes without written consent. Approved alterations must be restored at move-out or charges will apply.

9. LANDLORD ACCESS

9.1 Routine Inspections: Landlord may inspect quarterly with 48 hours written notice.

9.2 Repairs and Maintenance: Landlord may enter with 24 hours notice for repairs.

9.3 Showings: During final 60 days of Lease, Landlord may show property to prospective tenants with 24 hours notice, between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.

9.4 Emergency Entry: Landlord may enter immediately without notice for emergencies (fire, flood, gas leak, etc.).

10. TENANT OBLIGATIONS AND PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

10.1 Comply with all laws, HOA rules, and building regulations.

10.2 Dispose of trash properly in designated areas. Leaving trash in hallways results in $50 fine.

10.3 Report any infestations within 24 hours. Tenant is liable for pest control costs if infestation caused by Tenant's actions.

10.4 Prohibited: Waterbeds, space heaters, propane, fireworks, weapons discharge, illegal drugs, excessive guests, or any illegal activity.

10.5 No alterations to locks without Landlord's consent. Tenant must provide keys to all locks.

11. EARLY TERMINATION

11.1 Early Termination Fee: Tenant may terminate early with 60 days written notice and payment of:
- $3,000 early termination fee if terminating in months 1-6
- $2,000 early termination fee if terminating in months 7-9
- $1,000 early termination fee if terminating in months 10-12
Plus rent through the date Landlord re-rents the unit.

11.2 Military Clause: Active military members may terminate with 30 days notice and no penalty upon providing official deployment or PCS orders.

11.3 Landlord Termination: Landlord may terminate with 60 days notice if selling the property or moving in a family member.

11.4 Termination for Breach: Either party may terminate immediately upon material breach by the other party with 10 days written notice to cure.

12. SECURITY DEPOSIT DEDUCTIONS

Security deposit may be used for:
- Unpaid rent and fees
- Damage beyond normal wear and tear
- Cleaning (if not professionally done)
- Lock changes (if keys not returned)
- Unpaid utilities in Tenant's name
- Court costs and attorney fees

Normal wear and tear includes: minor scuffs, nail holes, carpet wear in traffic areas, faded paint.

Deductions do not include: repainting (unless excessive marks), carpet replacement (unless stained or damaged), appliance replacement (unless damaged by misuse).

13. RULES VIOLATIONS AND PENALTIES

13.1 First violation: Written warning
13.2 Second violation: $250 fine
13.3 Third violation: $500 fine and 30-day notice to vacate
13.4 Serious violations (illegal activity, safety hazards): immediate termination

SOFTWARE AS A SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Software as a Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 15, 2024, between CloudTech Solutions Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Provider"), and the subscribing customer ("Customer").

1. SERVICE DESCRIPTION AND FEES

1.1 Services: Provider agrees to provide Customer access to its cloud-based project management platform, including 100GB storage, unlimited users, and 24/7 technical support.

1.2 Subscription Fees: 
- Professional Plan: $149 per month
- Annual prepayment discount: 20% ($1,430.40 per year)
- Implementation fee: $500 (one-time, non-refundable)
- Overage charges: $0.50 per GB over 100GB storage limit

1.3 Payment Terms: All fees are due within 10 days of invoice date. Late payments will incur a 1.5% monthly interest charge.

2. TERM AND TERMINATION

2.1 Initial Term: This Agreement begins on March 15, 2024, and continues for an initial term of 24 months, ending March 14, 2026.

2.2 Renewal: This Agreement automatically renews for successive 12-month periods unless either party provides written notice at least 60 days prior to the renewal date.

2.3 Termination for Convenience: Customer may terminate this Agreement with 90 days written notice. Early termination will result in an early termination fee equal to 50% of the remaining contract value.

2.4 Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure within 30 days.

2.5 Data Retrieval: Upon termination, Customer has 30 days to retrieve all data. After 30 days, Provider may permanently delete all Customer data.

3. CUSTOMER OBLIGATIONS AND RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 Customer must designate an administrative contact within 5 business days of execution.

3.2 Customer must maintain current and accurate billing information at all times.

3.3 Customer is responsible for all activity under its account and must immediately notify Provider of any unauthorized access.

3.4 Customer must comply with Provider's Acceptable Use Policy, which prohibits illegal activities, spam, malware distribution, and excessive resource consumption.

3.5 Customer must maintain minimum of 3 active users throughout the term.

4. PROVIDER OBLIGATIONS AND SERVICE LEVELS

4.1 Uptime Guarantee: Provider guarantees 99.5% uptime per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

4.2 Scheduled Maintenance: Provider may perform maintenance with 72 hours notice, during 12 AM - 6 AM EST windows.

4.3 Service Credits: If uptime falls below 99.5%, Customer receives service credits: 
- 99.0% - 99.5%: 10% monthly fee credit
- 98.0% - 99.0%: 25% monthly fee credit
- Below 98.0%: 50% monthly fee credit

4.4 Support Response Times:
- Critical issues: 2 hours
- High priority: 8 business hours
- Medium priority: 24 business hours
- Low priority: 48 business hours
